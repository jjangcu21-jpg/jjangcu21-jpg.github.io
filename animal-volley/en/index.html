Before you hit the court

Where can I play?

Download the Android version on Google Play. Online matches require an internet connection.

Do I have to watch ads?

Rewarded ads are optional. You can enjoy the core game without watching them.

Which languages are supported?

Korean, English, Japanese, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Traditional Chinese and Hindi. The game starts in your device language, and you can change it in Settings.

Where is progress saved?

Progress is saved on your device. Recovery after uninstalling or changing devices is not currently supported.
